# Shloka Yatra 2027 — Customer & Devotee User Guide

Welcome to **Shloka Yatra 2027**. This guide provides step-by-step instructions for pilgrims seeking to explore sacred packages, register as Sangha Members, submit an 8-step booking, and manage their pilgrimage documents.

---

## 1. Exploring Pilgrimage Packages & 2027 Departures

1. Navigate to the public website homepage: [http://localhost:5173](http://localhost:5173).
2. Click **Travel Routes** or **Packages** in the top navigation to view the three sacred options:
   * **Option 1: Classic Overland (15 Days):** Travel by deluxe coach via Kathmandu, Kodari border, Nyalam, Saga, Lake Manasarovar, and Kailash Parikrama.
   * **Option 2: Fly to Lhasa, Continue by Road (14 Days):** Scheduled high-altitude flight to Lhasa with acclimatisation before joining the overland route.
   * **Option 3: Fly to Lhasa & Ali/Pulan (11 Days):** Scenic flight connecting Lhasa and Ngari Gunsa Airport for the shortest duration trek.
3. Review the day-by-day itineraries, sacred inclusions, acclimatisation schedules, and quotation details.

---

## 2. Devotee Membership Registration

Before submitting a booking, devotees register with the Shloka Yatra Sangha to ensure identity verification and document compliance:

1. Click **Become a Member** in the top header.
2. Enter your personal details:
   * **Full Name** (as per passport/national ID).
   * **Mobile Number** with country code (e.g. `+91 98765 43210`).
   * **Email Address**.
   * **Password** (min. 8 characters with at least one number and special character).
3. Verify your mobile number using the inline OTP verification code displayed during simulation.
4. Agree to the **Terms of Membership** and click **Create Devotee Account**.
5. Upon successful registration, you receive a unique membership number (format: `SHL-MEM-2027-XXXX`).

## 3. The 8-Step Booking Process

When booking a yatra, you proceed through the 8-step pilgrimage registration pipeline:

```
[1. Package] ➔ [2. Batch] ➔ [3. Contact] ➔ [4. Yatris] ➔ [5. Medical] ➔ [6. Documents] ➔ [7. Consent] ➔ [8. Payment]
```

* **Step 1 — Package Selection:** Choose your travel route and passport type (**Indian Passport** or **Foreign / NRI Passport**).
* **Step 2 — Batch Selection:** Select from the 8 official 2027 departure dates (including auspicious Vaishakh, Jyeshtha, and Ashadha Full Moon batches).
* **Step 3 — Primary Contact:** Enter your billing address, contact phone, and official email.
* **Step 4 — Yatri Details:** For each traveller, enter:
  * Full Name (as per Passport).
  * Passport / National ID Number.
  * Age (between 12 and 85 years).
  * Gender and Dietary Preference (Pure Vegetarian / Jain Vegetarian).
  * Emergency Contact Name and Phone.
* **Step 5 — Medical Fitness:** Complete altitude fitness declarations and affirm that a certified clinician will certify your high-altitude fitness.
* **Step 6 — Document Upload:** Upload simulated verification copies of your passport photo page, passport-size photo, medical certificate, and travel insurance.
* **Step 7 — Contractual Consents:** Affirmative acceptance of statutory policies:
  * Master Terms & Conditions (T1–T35).
  * Cancellation & Force Majeure Policy (C1–C17).
  * Package Service Schedule (Annex A).
  * High-Altitude Risk Acknowledgement (R1–R12).
  * Privacy & DPDP Notice (P1–P17).
* **Step 8 — Payment & Booking Finalization:**
  * Select your payment installment: **Registration Advance Only (₹25,000 / $250)** or **First 50% Package Instalment**.
  * Choose your payment method: UPI, Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking, or **Offline Payment (Testing Only)**.
  * Click **Complete Test Booking** / **Authorize Payment**.

## 4. Booking Reference & Success Confirmation

* Upon submission, the system generates an official booking reference code: `SHY-2027-XXXXXX`.
* Your seat reservation is immediately locked in the database.
* A summary confirmation page provides remittance instructions, departure dates, and yatri checklists.
